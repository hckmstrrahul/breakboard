# Two-phase hybrid stepper — geometry evidence

Purpose: interactive educational 3D teardown, presented vertically along its shaft. This is a **representative 42 mm hybrid stepper**, not the exact internals or exterior CAD of a named commercial model. Model units are millimetres; export is 2400 px wide, fitted rather than physical scale.

| Feature group | Evidence | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-phase magnetic construction | Manufacturer explanation | 8 stator poles, 4 per phase; two interleaved winding sets. |
| Rotor pole cups | Manufacturer explanation | Exactly 50 teeth per cup, 7.2° pitch, 3.6° angular offset between cups. |
| Full-step angle | Manufacturer explanation | 1.8°, 200 full steps per revolution. Play advances 100 full steps per second (30 rpm), for readable educational motion. |
| Axial permanent magnet | Manufacturer explanation | Annular magnet between the two rotor cups; opposite poles along the shaft. No alloy or flux-density claim. |
| Frame size | Commercial family reference | 42 mm square flange is representative of the documented PKP frame-size class. This is not branded as a PKP replica. |
| Body, mounting and shaft dimensions | Illustrative | 40 mm body length, 31 mm mounting centers and Ø5 shaft are representative choices. The output has a D-flat. |
| Stator teeth and laminations | Explanatory reconstruction | Pole shape, 5 teeth per pole face and lamination thickness/count are illustrative. Outer steel path and pole roots join physically. |
| Winding coils and bobbins | Explanatory reconstruction | 14 visible turns per coil are deliberately reduced for legibility; no actual resistance, inductance or current rating is claimed. Bobbins are hollow frames around the iron. |
| End bells and bearings | Explanatory reconstruction | Aluminum end bells with hollow interiors and bearing seats; two races, balls and cages. Ball count and bearing dimensions are illustrative. |
| Screws and connector | Explanatory reconstruction | Four through fasteners and a four-contact winding connector. Exact connector family, pin ordering and fastener series are unspecified. |

## Geometry and verification

The rotor axis is world y. Independent moving child groups for both cups, the magnet and shaft carry the same `motion.type = stepper`, preserving their relative angular offset through Play. Explosion translates their parent groups only. The 50-tooth profiles are generated from one definition, so repeated count and pitch cannot differ across views.

Assembled clearances in the illustrative geometry:

- Rotor tooth tip radius 12.25 mm; stator tooth inner radius 12.50 mm: 0.25 mm radial air gap.
- Shaft radius 2.50 mm matches the rotor and bearing bores.
- Pole body spans y=6…34 mm. Coil end turns sit beyond these ends; coil sides clear the 3.8 mm-wide iron cores. Bobbin end pieces are hollow, rather than solid plates crossing the iron.
- Rotor cups span y=8.5…19 and 21…31.5 mm. The magnet occupies 19…21 mm.
- Fastener centers coincide with rear-bell and stator holes and terminate in front-bell bosses. Four separate mounting holes remain visible on the front face.

Verified finite geometry, nonempty selectable groups and floor clearance over the full pose range. The actual application renderer was visually inspected assembled, separated and in anatomy mode. Unpublished internal tolerances and winding connections are not claimed.

Rebuild with `npm run build`; run `npm run check`. Inspect Stepper motor at 0%/50%/100% separation, in both themes and X-ray. In Play, the end bells and outer stator shell become translucent while the windings and pole teeth stay solid. The shaft and both cups turn together at 30 rpm while the stator stays still; isolate a winding to inspect the loop and bobbin construction.
