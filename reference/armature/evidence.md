## Behavior

Input current and frequency control reed displacement. The coil and magnets stay fixed. The formed rigid rod follows the reed tip and diaphragm with matching displacement at both endpoints, avoiding the magnetic yoke. Motion is enlarged and slowed by400×. Zero drive returns to neutral; Pause holds pose; Reset is reproducible; reduced motion is stationary. The normalized outlet-pressure waveform is an illustrative phase relationship over a2ms input window, not SPL or measured response. No resonance, distortion, armature saturation or acoustic coupling prediction is claimed.
