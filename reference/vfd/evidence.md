# Vacuum display — evidence and reconstruction

Fine filament wires and mesh grids sit above luminous phosphor segments.

## Supported construction

Noritake’s filament → control-grid → phosphor-anode stack, fused glass, vacuum leads, getter and evacuation stem. No particular eight-digit SKU or its dimensions is claimed.

## Reconstruction limits

Layer order, heated filaments, open control grids, phosphor anodes, glass frit, getter and evacuation stem follow Noritake technical documentation. The eight-digit layout, size, pin count, grid pitch and routing are representative and do not claim a specific Noritake SKU. Play slows multiplexing for visibility; the physical drive frequency is much higher.

All geometry is generated locally in [the model builder](../../src/vfd.js) from one component inventory. Realistic, anatomy, selected, isolated, assembled and exploded views share those exact meshes. These are educational assemblies, not manufacturing drawings or instructions for physically opening sealed devices.

## Play

Slowed multiplex scan · illustrative digit sequence. Covers marked as explanatory cutaways fade only during unselected realistic playback; selecting a part restores its normal material. Effects share the app clock, stop with separation, and use a still powered state under reduced motion. No measured optical, electrical or thermal output is claimed.
