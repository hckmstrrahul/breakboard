# R nixie tube — geometry evidence

Purpose: interactive educational 3D teardown in breakboard, using the same geometry in realistic and anatomy views. Dimensions are millimetres. This is a reconstruction, not manufacturing CAD. Normal export is 2400 px wide; the responsive canvas is fitted rather than drawn at a physical scale.

| Feature group | Evidence | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total tube height, numeral height | Supplied manufacturer dimensions | 125 mm including pins; each of ten glyph curves is normalized to 50 mm height. |
| Base envelope and external contacts | Supplied geometry | The official socket DXF gives an outer Ø53 envelope, Ø35 contact circle and 11 sockets. Circle centers are transcribed below. The Ø1.95 circles in the DXF are **socket drill holes**, not external pin diameters. |
| Glass cylinder, shoulder and tip | Visible in photographs | Hollow lathed shell, reconstructed 0.8 mm wall. The exact blower profile is unavailable. |
| Anode grid and rear plate | Manufacturing description and visible photographs | Folded perforated front and sides, rear metal plate with welded tabs. Hexagonal pitch, strip thickness and dimensions are reconstructed. |
| Ten numeral cathodes | Documented count; photograph-based reconstruction | Ten metal numeral strokes, 50 mm high. Letterforms, stack order and lead routes are illustrative. They are not a verified pinout. |
| Ceramic spacers and support tabs | Manufacturing description; unspecified detailed geometry | Ceramic sleeves isolate each metal cathode support tab from the longitudinal support rods. Hollow ring geometry preserves electrical isolation in the drawing. Exact ring dimensions and quantity are representative. |
| Mica supports | Manufacturing description and photographs | Two holders with holes; profile and thickness reconstructed. |
| Pinched stem, conductors, getter | Manufacturing description | Glass feedthrough and 11 conductors; ring getter on its support. Routing and dimensions illustrative. |
| Dark cylindrical base | Footprint and photographs | Ø53 outer envelope. Anodized appearance, base depth and internal carrier thickness reconstructed. |
| Operation | Documented cathode selection mechanism | One numeral glows at a time in Play; a slowed illustrative cycle, not voltage/current or plasma simulation. |

## Separation and checks

The envelope is a sealed part. Its separation is explanatory and does not imply that a real tube can be disassembled without destroying the seal. At full separation the ten cathodes form two parallel rows for legibility; the glass, grid, plate and insulators move to separate lanes. This presentation deliberately expands the stack beyond a literal single-axis withdrawal.

Verified: finite geometry; all 19 selectable groups are nonempty; assembled height 125 mm and base diameter 53 mm; ten independent indicator materials; all pose groups clear the scene floor. The real application renderer was inspected in assembled, exploded and anatomy states. Individual letterform curves are normalized before geometry construction. Glass meshes carry `anatomyTransparent`, preserving the electrode view in anatomy.

Rebuild with `npm run build`; validate collection contracts with `npm run check`. Inspect Nixie tube in the app, both themes, at 0%/50%/100% separation and in X-ray. Use Play to cycle the cathodes, and select/isolate a numeral to inspect its support and lead.
